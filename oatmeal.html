Peanut Butter in Oatmeal

A spoonful of peanut butter turns a plain bowl of oats into a warm, rich breakfast that keeps you full all morning.

What you need

  • 1/2 cup of rolled oats
  • 1 cup of milk or water
  • 1 tablespoon of peanut butter
  • Optional toppings: sliced banana, maple syrup, or a few chopped peanuts

How to make it

  1. Cook the oats with the milk or water for about 5 minutes, stirring often.
  2. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the peanut butter until it melts.
  3. Pour into a bowl and add your toppings.

My tip: make it the night before as overnight oats and eat it cold if you are in a hurry.
