Peanut Butter, Three Ways

Peanut butter is one of the easiest ways to make a simple meal taste better and keep you full longer. It is cheap, it lasts for months in the pantry, and it goes with sweet and savory food alike.

This site shows my three favorite ways to eat it. Each one takes less than ten minutes and uses ingredients most kitchens already have:

  • On toast – the classic, done right.
  • With yogurt – a creamy, high-protein bowl.
  • In oatmeal – a warm breakfast that lasts until lunch.
