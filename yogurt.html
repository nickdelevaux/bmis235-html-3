Peanut Butter with Yogurt

Stirring peanut butter into yogurt makes a thick, creamy bowl that tastes like dessert but is packed with protein. It works for breakfast, a snack, or after a workout.

What you need

  • 1 cup of plain or vanilla Greek yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon of peanut butter
  • Optional toppings: granola, berries, honey, or chocolate chips

How to make it

  1. Spoon the yogurt into a bowl.
  2. Add the peanut butter and swirl it in with a spoon. Do not mix it all the way, so you get ribbons of peanut butter.
  3. Top with granola and fruit for crunch.

My tip: warm the peanut butter for 10 seconds in the microwave so it drizzles easily.
