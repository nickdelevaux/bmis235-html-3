Peanut Butter on Toast

This is the classic, and it is a classic for a reason. Warm, crunchy bread melts the peanut butter just enough so it spreads into every corner.

What you need

  • 1 slice of hearty bread (whole wheat or sourdough)
  • 1–2 tablespoons of peanut butter
  • Optional toppings: sliced banana, a drizzle of honey, or a pinch of sea salt

How to make it

  1. Toast the bread until it is golden brown.
  2. Spread the peanut butter while the toast is still hot.
  3. Add your toppings and eat it right away.

My tip: banana slices with a little cinnamon on top turn this into a full breakfast.
